### Week 7 Objectives:

* Understand and implement High Availability (HA) and Auto Scaling models on AWS.
* Configure Elastic Load Balancer (ELB) and Auto Scaling Group (ASG) for EC2.
* Apply SQS/SNS to handle queues and notifications.
* Analyze logs and monitor network activity through VPC Flow Logs.
