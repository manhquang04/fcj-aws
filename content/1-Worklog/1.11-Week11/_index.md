* Study and practice Modernization & Serverless Architecture on AWS.
* Build, deploy, and manage Serverless applications with Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Cognito, and SAM.
* Understand how to decompose monolith into microservices to optimize scalability and maintainability.
